How it works

From the browser to the pedal in three steps

Nothing to install and no account needed to start. Sign in when you want your patches kept safe and on every device.

  1. Build the chain

    Pick up to 6 effects, drag them into order and set every parameter with the real ranges.

  2. Check it

    Watch the DSP load against the 100% limit and read the advice before you leave the desk.

  3. Send it to the pedal

    Download the .zptc file for Handy Guitar Lab, or connect the MS-60B+ by USB and write it from the Pedal manager.

Pedal manager · MS-60B+ · experimental

Your pedal and your patches, side by side

Connect the MS-60B+ with a USB cable and see the patches stored on the pedal next to the ones you saved, without Handy Guitar Lab.

  • Read every memory of the pedal and open a patch in the builder to edit it.
  • Write a patch to the memory you choose, or clear one.
  • Backup first: before a memory is overwritten its patch is kept, and you can restore it.
  • Checked: after writing, the memory is read back to confirm it.

Free account

Keep your patches safe

You can use everything without signing in, but then your work lives only in one browser. A free account keeps it on the server and brings it to every device where you sign in.

Without an account

  • Patches, settings and pedal backups stay only in this browser.
  • Clearing browsing data or a private window loses them.
  • Another browser or computer won't have them.

With a free account

  • Patches, settings, rule packs and pedal backups for both pedals are saved on the server.
  • The same library on every device where you sign in, synced on its own.
  • The patches already in your browser come with you when you sign up.
  • Download everything or delete your account whenever you want.

Features

What's inside

Everything works for both pedals unless it says otherwise.

Effects library

Every effect with the original ZOOM Effects List text, parameters and ranges. Search by effect, model or parameter and filter by category.

Patch builder

Up to 6 effects per patch, reorder by dragging or from the keyboard, undo and redo.

Live DSP meter

The load of each effect and of the whole chain, on the same scale as the Handy Guitar Lab "Processor" meter.

.zptc export

A file that imports into Handy Guitar Lab. On the MS-60B+ it round-trips byte for byte against real exports.

My patches

Save, compare two versions, back up and restore, share a link. Kept in your account when you sign in.

Pedal manager bass · experimental

Read and write the MS-60B+ memories over USB, with a backup before every write and a read-back check.

Free account

Your patches, settings and pedal backups on every device, for both pedals. Download or delete them any time.

Advice engine full on bass

Flags effect order, hot levels and heavy chains, and explains why with sources. The guitar version has the objective checks only.

Style profiles bass

Starter chains and advice tuned per playing style, adapted to a bass amp or a mixer.

Tone ideas bass

Starting points by style and by bassist, focused on alternative rock. Each one cites its sources and opens in the builder.

Works offline

Installable as an app on a computer or a tablet held sideways, so it works at rehearsals too.

Help wanted

Do you own an MS-50G+?

One quick test on a real pedal is enough to confirm the guitar version, or to show what needs fixing.

  1. Build a simple patch in the MS-50G+ builder and download the .zptc.
  2. Import it in Handy Guitar Lab and copy it to a free memory.
  3. Check the effects and values on the pedal, and tell us what you see. An exported patch from your pedal helps too.
