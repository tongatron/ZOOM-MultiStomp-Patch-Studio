Pedal over USB experimental

Reads patches straight from the pedal over USB MIDI, without Handy Guitar Lab. Read-only: nothing is written to the pedal. Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer; the browser asks for permission to use MIDI devices.

1. Connect

Connect the pedal with a USB data cable, switch it on and close Handy Guitar Lab and other MIDI apps.

Not connected

2. Read a patch

MIDI log

If something fails, copy the log and send it: it shows the bytes exchanged with the pedal.
