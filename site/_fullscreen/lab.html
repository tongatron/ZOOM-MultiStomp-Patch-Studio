Every page at every size

Opens each layout and page at each screen size and checks that nothing is cut: the page, the work area and every pane fit, and the main controls are on screen. On a phone the page scrolls on purpose for now (phone layout: phase 4 of the plan).

What to look at

  • At 1280 × 680 (a laptop with the browser bars) nothing is cut and no control is hidden.
  • Clicks for the usual round: find an effect, add it, set it, save and download the .zptc.
  • Text stays readable (13 px or more); on a tablet the buttons are easy to tap.
  • Which panes scroll, and whether that feels natural.
  • How much would change for the MS-50G+ pages and for Pedal manager, Tone ideas and Resources.
