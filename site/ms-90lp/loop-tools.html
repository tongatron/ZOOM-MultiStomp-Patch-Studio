On this page

    Loop tools

    Tools to plan loops for the ZOOM MS-90LP+, worked out from the tempo and the time signature. They do not talk to the pedal, and what you enter stays in this browser. The limits come from the manual: a tempo from 40 to 300 and up to 90 minutes for one loop.

    Loop length

    How long a loop of a number of bars lasts at a tempo.

    Tempo for a length

    The tempo at which a number of bars lasts a given time, for a loop that must be as long as a part of a song. Write the length in seconds or as minutes:seconds (1:30).

    Tap tempo and metronome

    Tap the button (or press T) along with the music: the tempo is the average of the last taps, and a pause of more than two seconds starts again. The metronome plays a plain click at a tempo, with the first beat of each bar higher. It is not one of the rhythm patterns of the pedal.

    — BPM

    Where the looper goes

    A looper records what reaches it. The effects before it are part of the loop; the effects after it are played over the loop as it plays back, and over what you play live.

    • At the end of the chain it records everything, so the loop keeps its sound when you change your pedals: this is the usual place.
    • Before the delay and the reverb, as in the BOSS guide to pedal order (with the reverb last), the delay and the reverb also act on the loop as it plays back.
    • In the middle of the chain, a wah, a distortion or a delay after the looper changes the loop while it plays.
    Sources Stringjoy: Guitar Pedal Order, How To Organize Your Pedalboard BOSS: The Ultimate Guide to Guitar Effects Pedal Order and Signal Chain Guitar World: 7 creative ways to use a looper pedal

    Ideas for the loop effects

    The Effects page has the official text of the 8 loop effects. A few ways to use them:

    • A pad with HALF SPEED: record a few chords and swell each one in with the volume knob or a volume pedal, so no pick attack is heard, then play the loop at half speed under what you play.
    • A phrase backwards with REVERSE: record a solo or an intro riff and play it back reversed, at its end or later in the song.
    • A short accent: REVERSE, HALF SPEED, DOUBLE SPEED and HOLD can be set to work only while you hold the LOOP FX switch (UNLATCH), so the loop changes for a moment and comes back by itself.
    Sources Guitar World: 7 creative ways to use a looper pedal ZOOM MS-90LP+ Operation Manual (UNLATCH)

    Song planner

    The parts of a song, each with a rhythm pattern, a tempo, a number of bars and a loop effect: the planner adds up the time. It is kept in this browser only; Print gives you a sheet to keep next to the pedal.
