    Effects

    The ZOOM MS-90LP+ is a looper, not a pedal with a chain of effects: this page lists what it has, with the original text of the ZOOM Operation Manual (the MS-90LP+ has no Effects List). There are 8 effects for the loop and 68 rhythm patterns; the pedal keeps 100 memories, each with one phrase of up to 90 minutes, its rhythm and its selected effect.

    Beta. These pages have not been checked on a real pedal. Own an MS-90LP+? Press Write to us in the bar at the top of the page.

    Loop effects

    Effects can be used during loop playback: turn the LOOP FX knob to select one and press the LOOP FX switch to turn it on and off.
