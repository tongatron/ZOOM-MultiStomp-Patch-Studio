    Help us test

    The MS-90LP+ pages are built from the ZOOM manual (the pedal has no Effects List), and nothing on the site talks to the pedal yet. These checks tell us whether the pages are right, and what the pedal shows when it is connected to a computer.

    Nobody is expected to do everything, and any single check helps. If you have the pedal, write to us with the time you could give: we organise the checks together, according to the time you have.

    The form asks for an email address so we can reply; the files come by email, as a reply.

    The short version: all three checks take about 35 minutes, and each one is useful on its own.

    What it would take

    • The MS-90LP+, and for check 3 a USB data cable and a computer.
    • The firmware version of the pedal: the version 1.10 supplement and the manual say what each version adds.

    Recording a loop in check 2 overwrites the memory you use: use a free memory.

    Check 1: the Effects page against the pedal

    About 10 minutes. Compare the Effects page with the pedal: the names of the 8 loop effects, and the number, name and time signature of five rhythm patterns of your choice. Tell us anything that is different or missing.

    Check 2: the Loop tools against the pedal

    About 10 minutes. Set a rhythm in 4/4 at 120 BPM and record a loop along with it, stopping after 4 bars. Then compare the length of the loop (the length of its WAV file over USB, in any audio player, or what the pedal shows) with Loop length: 8.0 seconds for 4 bars at 120 BPM. Tell us the length and whether the pedal rounded the loop to whole bars.

    Check 3: the pedal on a computer

    About 10 minutes. Connect the pedal to a computer with the USB cable, as the manual describes. We would need a screenshot of the folders and file names the computer shows, the size of one loop file, and, if you know how to check, whether the pedal also shows up as a MIDI device.

    What happens with the results

    The Effects page and the Loop tools can then be called checked, and what the pedal shows over USB tells us whether the site could one day read your loops and memories.
