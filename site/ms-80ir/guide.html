    Guide

    How to use ZOOM MultiStomp Studio with the ZOOM MS-80IR+, the amp and speaker-cabinet pedal of the MultiStomp series. There is nothing to install and you do not need an account. The site is made for a computer or a tablet held sideways: it needs a window of at least 800 × 640 px, and below that it shows a notice instead (try the full-screen mode of the browser, F11).

    Beta. The MS-80IR+ pages are new and have not been checked on a real pedal. There is the Effects page and a read-only Pedal manager: it reads the pedal and keeps backups, but it never writes to the pedal and there is no Patch builder yet, because the patch format of this pedal has not been checked on hardware. Own an MS-80IR+? Press Write to us in the bar at the top of the page.

    Find an effect

    The Effects page lists all 41 effects of the MS-80IR+ with the original text of the ZOOM Effects List: 23 amp and cabinet models, 5 studio ambiences, the IR effect for your own impulse responses, tools and delays.

    • Type in the search box at the top of the list, or press /: it looks in names, descriptions and parameter names.
    • Narrow the list with the Categories and Features filters on the left, change the order with the sort menu, and switch between list and grid with the two buttons above the list.
    • Click an effect to see it in the pane on the right: description and every parameter with its range.
    • Mark the effects you like with the star: Favorites on the left shows only them. They are kept in this browser.
    • The pedal plays one amp, one studio ambience and one other effect at the same time and keeps 80 patches (see the official manual).

    Read the pedal over USB

    The Pedal manager reads every memory of the MS-80IR+ and lists the patches with the effects it can name (the IR effect shows as an ID).

    • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, connect the pedal with a USB cable and press Connect; the browser asks once for MIDI access.
    • Back up all memories keeps a copy of the raw memories in this browser (the last 5); Download saves it as a file, and a single patch can be downloaded as .zptc.
    • Nothing is ever written to the pedal. Writing needs the patch format of this pedal to be checked on a real one.

    Troubleshooting

    • An effect shows "DSP not known yet". The IR effect is not in the firmware list the site uses, so its DSP load is not known.
    • The page says the window is too small. The site needs a window of at least 800 × 640 px: make it bigger or switch the browser to full screen (F11).
    • Something is wrong, or you miss something. Press Write to us at the top, or Contact us for bugs or anything in the bar at the bottom of any page: the message goes straight to the person who makes the site. Add your email if you would like an answer.
