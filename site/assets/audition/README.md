# Pedal audition samples

The built-in Pedal audition loops are real bass recordings downloaded from Pixabay and kept locally so the player also works offline after the service worker has cached them. They are used under the Pixabay Content License.

- `bass-guitar-groove-loop.mp3` — [Bass Guitar Groove Loop by Black_Kumizhi](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-bass-guitar-groove-loop-546981/)

- `electric-bass-groove-2-110.mp3` — [Electric bass guitar loop 4 by josefpres (Freesound)](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/musical-electric-bass-guitar-loop-4-bpm-110-43630/)
- `slap-bass-hit.mp3` — [Slap_bass by hkutluay (Freesound)](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/musical-slap-bass-90288/)

`funky-bass-daddy.mp3` is retained as an additional candidate for a future style slot: [Funky bass daddy by Darren_Hirst](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/musical-funky-bass-daddy-459571/).
